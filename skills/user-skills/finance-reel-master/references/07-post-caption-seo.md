# Stage 7: Post Caption, Hashtags, Cover & SEO

This stage outputs a ready-to-paste **publishing package**: caption, hashtags, cover text, alt text, location, pinned comment and cross-post variants.

In 2025–2026, discovery comes from **search and sends**, not hashtag volume:
- Instagram caps posts at **5 hashtags**, and Adam Mosseri, who runs Instagram, has said hashtags don't meaningfully increase reach.
- Instagram search reads captions.
- Since July 2025, public posts from professional accounts can appear in Google search.

Write for search and for sharing.

## Core principles

- **The keyword comes first.** The primary keyword phrase appears in caption line 1, is spoken in the VO, and is shown on screen.
- **Source every number** in the caption. Credibility is the brand.
- **One CTA, aimed at sends or saves.**
- **Never** use generic tags (#viral, #reels, #explore, #fyp). They add nothing.

## Step 7.1: Keyword research (2 minutes)

1. Type the topic into Instagram search and Google. Note the autocomplete phrases people actually use, e.g., "fixed deposit rates Sri Lanka", "AWPR", "Sri Lanka budget 2027 tax".
2. Choose **one primary keyword phrase** and 1–2 secondary phrases.
3. Make sure the primary phrase appears in four places: caption line 1, VO, on-screen text, and alt text where available.

## Step 7.2: Caption template

```
{Line 1: primary keyword + curiosity, the first ~125 characters show before "more"}

{2–3 takeaways, one line each, plain language}
• ...
• ...
• ...

Source: {primary source, publication/table, date}

{CTA: one only}
📩 Send this to someone who {specific situation}
or 🔖 Save this before {specific moment}

{Series line: Part of #{SeriesTag}, Ep. {N}. Part {N+1} drops {day}.}
{Disclaimer if investing/personal finance: Educational content, not financial advice.}

{3–5 hashtags}
```

- **Length:** 80–200 words for explainers (Instagram's limit is 2,200 characters).
- **Tone:** confident, plain, no hype words ("insane", "shocking") unless the number earns it.
- **Language:** match the VO language. For bilingual reels, write line 1 in the VO language and a one-line summary in the other.

**Example (English, numbers illustrative):**

```
Why your fixed deposit might be losing money in Sri Lanka, explained with one chart.

• A {X}% FD rate minus {Y}% inflation = your real return
• When inflation beats your rate, your savings buy less each year
• Check the "real" rate before you renew

Source: CBSL rates & DCS CCPI, {month year}

📩 Send this to someone renewing an FD this month.

Part of #EchelonExplains, Ep. 4. Next: why leasing rates are higher than loan rates.
Educational content, not financial advice.

#EchelonExplains #SriLankaEconomy #PersonalFinance #FixedDeposit
```

## Step 7.3: Hashtags (3–5, no more)

| Slot | Purpose | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Branded series tag (consistent, owned) | #EchelonExplains |
| 2 | Market/topic tag | #SriLankaEconomy / #SriLankaBusiness |
| 3 | Specific topic tag | #FixedDeposit / #InterestRates / #CSE |
| 4 (optional) | Audience/format tag | #PersonalFinance / #BusinessExplained |
| 5 (optional) | Event tag when news-jacking | #Budget2027 |

Check each tag in Instagram search before adopting it into a standard set: it should be active and relevant, not spammy. Keep a tag bank per series.

## Step 7.4: Cover / thumbnail

- **Design:** 1080×1920. One big number, or a 3–5 word title.
- **Placement:** key text inside y 285–1635 (it survives the 4:5 feed crop and the 3:4 grid crop) and inside x 65–950.
- **Series consistency:** same template, position and colour across a series, so the grid reads as a library.
- **Number on the cover:** the cover number and the hook number should be the same.
- **Tools:** Canva or After Effects frame export.

## Step 7.5: Alt text, location, collabs, pinned comment

- **Alt text** (where Instagram offers it under Advanced settings → Accessibility; always for carousel cut-downs): "Animated chart showing {what} from {source}, {period}. {Primary keyword}."
- **Location:** tag the city or country (e.g., Colombo, Sri Lanka) for local discovery.
- **Collab posts:** use with partner brands, institutions or creators when editorially appropriate; both audiences see the post. Sponsored content must use the **Paid partnership** label, and editorial claims still need sources.
- **Pinned comment** (post it yourself, then pin), using one of:
  - Full source links / table references.
  - "Part 2 is here → {link}".
  - A question that invites useful comments: "Fixed or floating, which is your loan?"
- **First hour:** reply to early comments with substance. Correct any error publicly and quickly, and pin the correction if it's material.

## Step 7.6: Cross-post variants

- **Facebook Reels:** same caption. Hashtags are optional.
- **YouTube Shorts:**
  - Title of 100 characters or fewer, with the keyword first: "Why Your FD Might Be Losing Money (Sri Lanka) #Shorts".
  - Description: 2–3 lines plus the source.
  - Swap "send this" for "share this".
- **LinkedIn** (for B2B topics): a 2–3 line professional framing plus the source. The native upload is the same master.
